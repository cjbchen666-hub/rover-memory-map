### 1. 拜占庭不是被奥斯曼打崩的，是先被自己掏空
- **事实**：军区制瓦解→小农被大地主兼并→中央税基萎缩→依赖雇佣兵和拉丁盟友；1204 第四次十字军本应去打埃及，因债务和皇位纠纷转洗君士坦丁堡；1453 只剩 7000 守军对 8 万奥斯曼。
- **原文摘录**："The most devastating single blow to Byzantine power…was inflicted not by traditional enemies from the east, but by Latin Christian crusaders from the west…Through a mixture of debt, political intrigue, and shifting promises, the crusading army was diverted to attack Christian targets." / "The Empire began to crumble…when it failed to protect the interests of small land holders, urban skilled workers and merchants."
- **来源**：https://kahibaro.com/course/74-history/6312-byzantine-decline ；https://ccj.pku.edu.cn/Article/DownLoad?id=271012644&&type=ArticleFile
- **可信度**：高（教学资料+北大期刊）。
- **所以呢**：城墙再厚也只是把死亡推迟；系统寿命取决于和"税基/负熵流"是否还通，不是取决于防御工事。

### 2. xkcd《How Old》：社交规则本身方向不对称
- **事实**：介绍孩子时家长被问"几岁"是礼貌，介绍他爹时被问"几岁"就是冒犯；title text 同一手势对孩子向上长、对老人向下缩。
- **原文摘录**："Interaction tip: This is a common question to ask parents about their kids, but for some reason in the other direction it's weird." / "For kids this usually means they have grown taller, but old people…will over time become more compressed and lose height."
- **来源**：https://www.explainxkcd.com/wiki/index.php/2213:_How_Old
- **可信度**：高。
- **所以呢**：规则不是对称的，箭头方向定语义——和 Rust 借用方向、耗散结构 deS 只能流入同构；评论区 ln(age) 补了尺度：主观时间天然对数刻度。

### 3. 海关用 GAN 造假贸易数据：拆成身份层和分布层
- **事实**：真实报关数据因隐私不能公开，用 conditional tabular GAN 造 54000 条/22 属性，保持联合分布，做 fraud detection 基准。
- **原文摘录**："limited accessibility of the transaction-level trade datasets hinders…open research…54,000 artificially generated trades with 22 key attributes…synthesized with conditional tabular GAN while maintaining correlated features."
- **来源**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02484
- **可信度**：高。
- **所以呢**：合成数据不是"假数据"，是只保留分布、丢弃个体——把数据拆成身份层（销毁）和分布层（保留），和钙成像把空间身份退成稀疏系数同构。
