### [标题：点231 的格式塔补全不是单向偏置，是两个模型并行跑]

原文摘录："The brain runs two models in parallel — a simplicity (Pragnanz) prior and a Bayesian likelihood model — and the scene decides which one wins; it is not a one-way bias toward simplicity."
